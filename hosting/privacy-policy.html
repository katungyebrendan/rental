Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rentals App ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create or use an account, we collect your email address and authentication information. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored in the app database.

Profile information. You may provide a name or business name, phone number, role, and profile photo. This information can be displayed to people viewing your property listings.

Property and activity information. We collect property details, listing photos, location and rental information that you submit. We also store saved-property choices and other actions needed to provide the Service.

Device and technical information. Firebase and our hosting infrastructure may receive technical information such as IP address, device or browser information, app version, crash information, and timestamps as part of operating, securing, and diagnosing the Service.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, publish and display listings, connect renters with listing owners, save your preferences, provide support, prevent abuse, maintain and improve the Service, and comply with legal obligations.

3. How information is shared

Listing and profile information that you choose to publish may be visible to other users. If you choose to contact a listing owner through WhatsApp or another external service, that interaction is governed by the other service's privacy policy.

We use service providers to operate the Service, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Hosting, and related Google services. They process information on our behalf under their applicable terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer.

4. Storage, security, and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information while your account or listing is active and for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, legal requirements, dispute resolution, and enforcement of our agreements.

5. Your choices and rights

You may review or update profile information in the app, remove saved properties, and request account or data deletion by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, or object to certain processing. We may need to verify your request before completing it. Deleting information may limit or disable parts of the Service.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes or legal requirements develop. We will post the revised version here and update the effective date. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to your use of the Service.
